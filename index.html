About the house

Ashirbad Girls House is a girls-only PG on Ramkrishnapur Road, off Jessore Road in Barasat. It is a short distance from Brainware University, which suits students as well as working women.

Rooms are air conditioned, with large windows and natural light. A watchman is on duty around the clock and CCTV covers the property.

Rooms and facilities

Shared room

Air-conditioned rooms with wooden bed frames. Residents bring their own mattress. Eleven beds are currently available.

Private room

A single air-conditioned room for one resident. There is one private room, so ask early if you want it.
